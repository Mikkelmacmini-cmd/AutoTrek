Complete Transmission Services

From routine fluid maintenance to full diagnostic and repair — AutoTrek handles every aspect of transmission service in-house.

Transmission Fluid Flush

Full system flush using a machine to replace nearly 100% of the old fluid — including what's in the torque converter and cooler lines — with the correct manufacturer-specified fluid.

Filter Replacement

Transmission pan dropped, pan cleaned, and filter replaced to prevent debris from circulating through the valve body and damaging internal components.

Computerized Diagnostics

Transmission-specific fault code scanning and live data analysis to identify solenoid failures, sensor issues, torque converter problems, and shift quality faults before they become major failures.

Solenoid & Sensor Repair

Faulty shift solenoids and speed sensors cause erratic shifting and transmission codes. We test and replace these targeted components to restore smooth operation at a fraction of rebuild cost.

Fluid Leak Repair

Transmission fluid leaks from pan gaskets, axle seals, output shaft seals, and cooler lines repaired to prevent fluid loss and overheating damage.

Road Test & Verification

Every transmission service includes a road test and post-service scan to confirm smooth shifting, no fault codes, and proper transmission operation across all gear ranges.

Why It Matters

Your Transmission Is Your Second Most Expensive Component

After the engine, the transmission is the most expensive component in your vehicle — costing $1,500–$5,000 or more to rebuild or replace. Yet most drivers never service it until something goes wrong. Transmission fluid degrades over time, losing its ability to lubricate, cool, and hydraulically actuate internal components.

A $150–$250 fluid service at AutoTrek every 30,000–60,000 miles is the single best investment you can make to prevent a catastrophic transmission failure. Colorado's mountain driving and extreme temperature swings make regular fluid maintenance even more critical here than in other states.

Warning Signs

Signs Your Transmission Needs Service

Slipping gears — the transmission shifts unexpectedly, pops out of gear, or the engine revs without power delivery.

Delayed or rough engagement — a lag or clunk when shifting from Park into Drive or Reverse.

Shuddering or jerking during shifts — worn fluid, a failing torque converter, or solenoid issues.

Burning smell — overheating or severely degraded transmission fluid. Have this checked immediately.

Reddish fluid puddle under vehicle — transmission fluid leak from a seal, gasket, or cooler line.

Transmission warning light or check engine light — a fault code is stored. Needs diagnostic scan immediately.
