What's Included

Complete Tire Services

From a simple rotation to a full four-wheel alignment — AutoTrek handles every aspect of tire maintenance and service in one visit.

Tire Rotation

Moving tires to different positions on your vehicle distributes wear evenly across all four tires, extending their life by 20–30%. Recommended every 5,000–7,500 miles.

Tire Balancing

We use computer-controlled balancing machines to detect and correct weight imbalances, eliminating vibration at highway speeds and preventing premature wear patterns.

Wheel Alignment

Precision computerized alignment adjusts camber, toe, and caster angles to manufacturer specifications. Corrects pulling, reduces tire wear, and improves fuel economy.

Tire Mounting

New tire mounting for tires purchased anywhere — or we can source the correct tire for your vehicle. All sizes, brands, and vehicle types serviced.

Tire Pressure Check

All tires inflated to manufacturer spec PSI. Proper inflation reduces rolling resistance, improves fuel economy, and prevents blowouts and uneven wear.

Tread Depth Inspection

We measure tread depth on all four tires and advise when replacement is needed. Tires below 2/32" tread depth are unsafe and should be replaced immediately.

Why It Matters

Proper Tire Care Saves Money & Lives

Tires are your vehicle's only contact point with the road. Misaligned wheels cause tires to wear unevenly and can cost you a full set of tires thousands of miles early. Unbalanced tires create dangerous vibration and stress on wheel bearings and suspension components.

Colorado's potholes, curbs, and temperature swings knock alignment out faster than most states. AutoTrek recommends an alignment check at least once a year — or immediately after any significant road impact. A proper alignment pays for itself in tire savings within the first year.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Tire Service

Vehicle pulling to one side — a classic sign of wheel misalignment. The vehicle drifts when you let go of the wheel.

Steering wheel vibration at highway speeds — typically caused by unbalanced tires or bent wheels.

Uneven or rapid tire wear — cupping, feathering, or wear on one edge indicates alignment or balance problems.

Crooked steering wheel — when driving straight your steering wheel is off-center, alignment is needed.

Tires approaching 5,000–7,500 miles since last rotation — time for a rotation to equalize wear across all four corners.
