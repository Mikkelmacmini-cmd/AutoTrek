Complete Brake Services

Every brake job at AutoTrek starts with a thorough inspection. We service the full system — not just the pads — so your brakes perform as designed.

Brake Pad Replacement

OEM-quality or performance pads for all makes and models. We check wear indicators and replace before metal-on-metal damage occurs.

Rotor Resurfacing & Replacement

We measure rotor thickness and resurface when specs allow, or replace when rotors are warped, scored, or below minimum thickness.

Caliper Service & Replacement

Sticking or seized calipers cause uneven wear and pulling. We inspect, clean, lubricate, and replace calipers as needed.

Brake Fluid Flush

Old brake fluid absorbs moisture and reduces stopping effectiveness. We flush and replace with fresh DOT-rated fluid to restore hydraulic pressure.

ABS System Inspection

We scan for ABS fault codes and inspect wheel speed sensors to ensure your anti-lock braking system is functioning correctly.

Full System Inspection

Brake lines, hoses, master cylinder, and parking brake are all checked. You receive a written digital report of all findings.

Why It Matters

Your Brakes Are Your Most Important Safety System

At highway speeds, proper brake function is the difference between a close call and a collision. Worn pads generate excessive heat, warp rotors, and dramatically increase stopping distances. What starts as a small repair becomes a much larger — and more dangerous — problem if ignored.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Brake Service

Squealing or squeaking — the wear indicator is contacting the rotor. Replace pads soon.

Grinding metal-on-metal sound — pads are fully worn. Rotor damage is likely occurring.

Vibration or pulsating pedal — rotors may be warped from heat stress.

Soft or spongy brake pedal — often indicates air in the brake lines or a fluid leak.

Vehicle pulling to one side — a sticking caliper or uneven pad wear is the typical cause.

Brake warning light illuminated — have your system inspected immediately.
